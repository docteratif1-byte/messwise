# Messwise

Shared tea and kitchen expenses for hostels, offices and shared flats. Android app by **KodaWick**.

- Tea Bill for everyone on tea: rate per cup = tea expenses ÷ cups made; each bill = cups × rate.
- Kitchen Bill only for those who share the kitchen, by days present (leave days skipped). Meals only for those who ate.
- Personal guests go to their host; office guests are divided among everyone.
- Parties with share per head and paid/pending, one-tap private tea marks, pending bills visible to all, payment slips, manager-only "paid".
- Six reports in PDF and Excel. Private expense book. English and Urdu.

Stack: Kotlin + Jetpack Compose (`app/`, `core/`), Supabase free plan (`supabase/`). Build and release notes: `docs/RELEASE.md`.
