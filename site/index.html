MessWise

Shared tea and kitchen bills, worked out fairly

MessWise keeps the shared tea and kitchen account of a hostel, office or shared flat open to everyone in the group.

Tea Bill for everyone on tea: no fixed price. At month end, rate per cup = that month's total tea expenses ÷ all cups made, and each bill = cups × that rate. The rate changes every month with what was spent and drunk.
Kitchen Bill only for those who share the kitchen: oil, flour and gas divided by days present, skipping leave. Meal bills go only to those who ate. Personal guests go to their host; office guests are shared by all.
Fair and open: every edit keeps its reason, pending bills are visible to everyone, payments carry a slip, and only the canteen manager marks a bill paid. Six reports in PDF and Excel. A private expense book only you can see.

Available on Google Play for Android. English and Urdu.
