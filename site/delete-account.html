Delete your MessWise account

In the app: open MessWise → More → Settings → Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. It takes effect at once.
By email: write from the Gmail you sign in with to kodawick4+messwise@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my MessWise account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your sign-in, your profile, your private expense book with its receipt photos, your private tea marks and your notifications.

What stays

Shared group records you were part of (entries, bills, payments and their history) stay, shown as "Former member", because other members' bills depend on them. If you own a group that still has members, hand over ownership first.
