Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. MessWise is made by KodaWick, Pakistan. Contact: kodawick4+messwise@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your name and email from Google sign-in.
  • Group records: the hostels or offices you join, your role, leave dates, tea counts, purchases, meals, parties, bills and payment records entered by you or your managers.
  • Photos you upload: receipts and payment slips, stored privately and shown only to members of that group through short-lived links.
  • Private book: your own expenses and budget. Only you can see them; managers and owners cannot.
  • Tea marks: your one-tap "Add Tea" marks are private to you unless you choose to share your tally with a manager when asking a question.

What we do not collect

No location, contacts, SMS or call logs. No card or bank numbers. No advertising or analytics SDKs. MessWise never holds or moves money.

Who can see what

Members of a group see that group's entries, bills, pending amounts and the history of changes. Questions to managers are visible only to you and the managers. Nothing is shared with third parties or sold.

Where data is kept

On Supabase servers, encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Every table is protected by row-level security and the app reaches data only through checked server functions.

Notifications

The app checks for new bills, reminders and notices about once an hour while you are signed in. You can turn this off in Settings.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Or see delete your account. Your login, profile and private book are deleted. Shared bills and entries stay in the group's records, shown as "Former member", so the group's accounts still add up. An owner of a group with other members must hand over ownership first.

Children

MessWise is meant for adults and older students living or working together. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above if anything changes.
